SERVICES

What can Solely Social help with?

Choose one area or connect multiple pieces of your marketing.

What services do you offer?

Solely Social Marketing focuses on social media, email marketing, and digital strategy. Support can include content planning, creative direction, branded content, captions, email campaigns, welcome sequences, promotional emails, content calendars, audience-focused strategy, reporting, and optimization recommendations.

Do you only manage social media?

No. Social media is one part of the bigger picture. I also help with email marketing and digital strategy so your channels can support the same overall goals instead of feeling disconnected.

Can I hire you for just one service?

Yes. You do not have to hire me for everything. We can focus on the area where you need the most support, whether that is social content, email marketing, strategy, or a combination.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Solely Social is built with growing businesses in mind, especially businesses that want thoughtful, professional marketing without having to build a full in-house marketing department.

Can you work with my existing brand?

Yes. We can build from what you already have, refine your visual and messaging direction, or create a more cohesive approach when your current marketing feels scattered.

Can you help if I don’t know what I need?

Absolutely. You do not need to arrive with a perfect marketing plan. Tell me what feels stuck, what you want to improve, and what you are hoping to accomplish, and we can identify a practical place to start.

THE PROCESS

What is it like to work together?

A simple process designed to keep things clear and manageable.

What happens after I contact you?

We start with a conversation about your business, audience, goals, and what you would like to improve. From there, we can identify the right type of support and outline the next step.

Do I need to know exactly what I want before reaching out?

No. You can come with a specific project, a general goal, a messy list of marketing problems, or simply the feeling that your current marketing needs a refresh. We can figure out the direction together.

How involved will I need to be?

That depends on the project and type of support you choose. I’ll need your input to understand your business and brand, while the amount of ongoing collaboration can be shaped around the work we are doing together.

Will my marketing sound like me?

That is the goal. Your business has its own personality, and the strategy, content, and messaging should reflect it. I focus on understanding your voice and audience before creating.

Do you use templates or cookie-cutter strategies?

I use proven marketing principles and efficient systems where helpful, but your strategy and creative direction should be specific to your business, audience, goals, and brand personality.

How do we know what is working?

We can review relevant performance information and use what we learn to refine content, messaging, campaigns, and priorities. The specific measurements depend on your goals and the services involved.

EMAIL MARKETING

Questions about email?

Thoughtful emails can turn an audience into an ongoing relationship.

What kinds of emails can you create?

Depending on your needs, email support can include newsletters, promotional campaigns, welcome sequences, customer journeys, subject-line concepts, calls-to-action, and email content designed to match your broader marketing direction.

Can you help with a welcome email sequence?

Yes. A welcome sequence can introduce your brand, build trust, provide helpful information, and guide new subscribers toward an appropriate next step.

What if I do not have a large email list?

You can still benefit from having a thoughtful email foundation. The focus is on creating useful, relevant communication and building a relationship with the people who choose to hear from your brand.

Can email marketing work with social media?

Yes. Social and email can support each other. Social content can introduce people to your brand while email gives you a more direct way to nurture the relationship over time.

GETTING STARTED

Okay, how do I begin?

No pressure — just a simple first step.

How do I get started?

Use the contact form on the website and tell me a little about your business, what you need help with, and what you would like your marketing to accomplish. I’ll use that information to understand where to begin.

What should I include in my message?

A quick overview of your business, what you offer, who you serve, what you are currently doing for marketing, and what you would like to improve is a great place to start. You do not need to write a perfect brief.

What if I am not ready to commit?

That is okay. The first step is simply a conversation. You can ask questions and learn more about the process before deciding whether working together makes sense for you.

What if my question is not listed here?

Reach out and ask. Every business is different, so there may be something specific to your situation that is not covered on this page.
