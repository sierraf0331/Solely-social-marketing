What services do you offer?

I offer social media marketing, email marketing, and digital strategy. Depending on your needs, we can focus on one area or connect several pieces into a more complete marketing approach.

Do I have to hire you for everything?

No. You can get support with the specific area where your business needs the most help. We can keep it focused or build a broader plan together.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Solely Social is built with growing businesses and service providers in mind — especially businesses that want thoughtful marketing without feeling like they need a giant agency.

Can you help if I don’t know what I need?

Absolutely. You don’t need to arrive with a perfect marketing plan. Tell me what feels stuck, what you’re trying to accomplish, and what you’ve already tried. We can figure out where to begin.

Do you only do social media?

No. Social media is one part of what I do. Email marketing and digital strategy can work alongside social content to create a more connected customer experience.

What happens after I contact you?

First, you’ll tell me a little about your business and what you’re looking for. Then we’ll have a conversation about your goals and the kind of support that makes sense. From there, we can map out the next step.

Will you make my marketing sound like everyone else’s?

No. My goal is to understand what makes your business yours and turn that into a recognizable voice, visual direction, and marketing approach.

Can I ask for your 3 quick marketing ideas?

Yes! Mention “3 ideas” in your message through the contact form and I’ll know exactly what you mean.

What if I have another question?

Ask me! There’s no need to squeeze your question into a category. Use the contact form and tell me what you’re wondering about.
